Frequently asked questions

Answers to common CRM questions.

What is CRM?

A CRM is a system for managing customer relationships, sales pipelines, and team communications in one unified platform.

How does automation help?

Automation reduces manual tasks, improves follow-up speed, and ensures every opportunity is tracked consistently.

Is setup easy?

Yes — NexaCRM includes guided onboarding, templates, and support to get your team live in days.

Can teams collaborate?

Absolutely. Teams can share notes, tasks, and account histories to keep everyone aligned on customer success.

Is customer support available?

Yes — we offer dedicated support, knowledge resources, and onboarding assistance for all customers.
